## 🔍 About

**Saudi Real Estate Analysis** is a data science project that examines over **810,000 property listings** across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. By leveraging large-scale data processing and visualization, this project uncovers the structural dynamics of the Saudi property market, identifying key geographical hubs, pricing trends, and market behaviors.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Scale** — Processing a massive dataset of 810k+ real-time market entries.
- 🗺️ **Geospatial Insights** — Mapping market concentration across the Kingdom’s cities.
- 📈 **Trend Modeling** — Identifying price correlations between size, location, and property type.
- 🧪 **Data Engineering** — Advanced handling of outliers and Arabic text processing for real estate data.

## 💡 Strategic Market Insights

### 1. Market Culture & Ownership Trends 🏛️
* **The Ownership Culture:** The market is overwhelmingly dominated by sales listings (**77.8%**), significantly outperforming rentals (**22.2%**). This stark disparity highlights the deeply ingrained culture of property ownership and real estate investment in the Kingdom.

### 2. Geographical Dominance 🏙️
* **Metropolitan Concentration:** The "Big Four" cities (**Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, and Khobar**) command over **80%** of the entire market's activity.
* **The Capital Influence:** Riyadh stands as the undisputed epicenter of the real estate landscape, maintaining a massive gap in listing volume compared to all other regions.
* **Regional Disparity:** There is a noticeable decline in activity in border regions such as **Al-Jouf, Northern Borders, and Najran**, indicating that market liquidity is heavily centralized.

### 3. Urban Behavioral Patterns 🎯
* **The Rental Hub:** Riyadh records the highest rental rates, directly reflecting the city's high density of professionals and expatriates.
* **Sales-Centric Zones:** Cities like Buraydah, Al-Hofuf, and Taif are predominantly driven by sales-centric market activity.
* **Balanced Ecosystem:** Jeddah maintains the most balanced real estate environment, showing a healthy equilibrium between supply for sale and for rent.

### 4. Pricing & Asset Dynamics 💰
* **Valuation Gap:** Sales prices maintain significantly higher averages across all property categories.
* **Premium Segments:** **Buildings (Amayer)** and **Farms** are the most expensive assets, frequently exceeding **1 Million SAR**, with substantial price outliers.
* **Volatility in Land:** "Land" shows the highest variance, with values ranging from thousands to billions of Riyals, making it the most speculative segment.
* **Rental-Only Assets:** Chalets, Camps, and Rooms are exclusively listed for rent. **Rooms and Camps** represent the entry-level market with costs often below **1,000 SAR**.
